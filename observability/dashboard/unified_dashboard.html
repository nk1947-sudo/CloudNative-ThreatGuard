Dual-Track Remediation Recommendations (Dry-Run Only)

[Track 1: Cloud IAM Remediation]

Remove wildcard iam:PassRole permission from developer policy and restrict to explicit role ARNs.

[Track 2: Kubernetes Workload Remediation]

Quarantine affected workload by applying default-deny zero-trust network isolation label.

kubectl label pod threatguard-target-pod-12345 -n threatguard security.threatguard.io/quarantine=true --dry-run=client
